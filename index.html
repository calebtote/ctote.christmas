01 / Platform engineering

Designed & directed

Deployments that take minutes.

I designed a new cloud ecosystem at Gearbox and directed the migration. The work brought delivery and observability into a shared set of tools.

Deployment time dropped from hours to minutes. Most individual transitions now take about 60 seconds from the deployment trigger. That covers merge request to dev, dev to stable, or stable to production.

Inside the transformation +
  • Perforce and Bamboo → GitLab and GitLab CI/CD.
  • CloudFormation → Terraform.
  • Consul → AWS Secrets Manager.
  • Fragmented logging and metrics → unified, cost-tracked observability in Datadog across the stack.

Our original two-person DevOps team grew into a cloud infrastructure team. New hires already knew the tools we had adopted, which made onboarding easier.
